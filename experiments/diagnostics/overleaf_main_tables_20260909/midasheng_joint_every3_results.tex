\subsection{Preliminary Dual-Learning Results}

We report preliminary results for the original DualISL configuration, pairing
MiDashengLM-7B with Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign.
Table~\ref{tab:midasheng-joint-every3} tracks both models at three-round intervals.

\input{midasheng_joint_every3_table}

After nine rounds, the ParaSpeechCaps composite score increases from 0.2338
to 0.3010 (+28.7\% relative), while StyleCap accuracy improves by 0.86
percentage points. These results indicate improved captioner performance.
The available round-3 TTS results show higher WER and slightly lower WVMOS
than Base; later-round TTS evaluations are pending.
